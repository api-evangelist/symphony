MUFG Bank has launched a full-scale initiative to transform client communication in its wealth management business for high-net-worth clients. By adopting the technology of Symphony, a U.S. company that provides a secure communication platform purpose-built for the financial industry, the bank aims to modernize a wealth management sales practice that has, until now, relied primarily on email and phone calls.
